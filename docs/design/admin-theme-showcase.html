Project theme: every shadcn component in your palette

A working design-system reference: the components actually click and show their real behavior, not just their looks. New functionality is assembled from these pieces, so there's no need to revisit the styling. Badges and other small filled elements use darkened color versions — plain teal and green don't give white text enough contrast.

Cards and blocks have no borders — a white surface against the --background fill separates itself. A border only remains on form fields, tables, the dashed placeholder, and internal dividers (a card's footer, a menu separator).

Accordion — accordion

Score = tickets × 10000 + seconds waited. A comment gives 1 ticket, a gift gives tickets and pendingCoins based on its tier.
The overlay holds the last state it received and doesn't compute game logic itself. The server reconnects the socket automatically.
Day and month are a groupBy over a window from the round-participants table; "all-time" is read from the aggregate PlayerTotal table, with no on-the-fly recalculation.
Likes accumulate in the room's bank and, once a threshold is reached, trigger a global event — for example boost, which hands out pickups to everyone on the field.
